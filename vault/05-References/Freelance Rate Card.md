# Freelance Rate Card

Last updated: February 2026

---

## Current Rates

### Long-Form Features (2000+ words)
$0.40-0.60/word depending on complexity, research required, and publication prestige.
- High end ($0.60) for pieces requiring original reporting, interviews, or deep research
- Mid range ($0.50) for well-researched features with existing source material
- Low end ($0.40) for straightforward long-form with minimal original research

### Blog Posts (1000-2000 words)
$300-500/post for one-off pieces
$250-400/post on retainer (4+ posts/month)
- Retainer clients get priority scheduling and faster turnaround
- Price varies based on research depth, SEO requirements, and revision rounds included

### Newsletter / Email Content
$200-400/email depending on length and complexity
- Short emails (300-500 words): $200
- Full newsletter issues (800-1200 words): $300-400
- Includes one round of revisions

### Content Strategy Consulting
$150/hour
- Minimum 2-hour engagement
- Includes: editorial calendar planning, content audits, voice/brand development, workflow consulting
- Package rate available for ongoing advisory ($500/month for up to 4 hours)

### Rush Fee
+25% on top of standard rate for anything needed in less than 48 hours.
Non-negotiable. If they need it rushed, they can pay for it.

## Notes on Pricing

**When to raise rates:**
- After every 3rd new client at current rates — if demand is high, rates are too low
- Annual review every January (did this in Jan 2026, raised blog posts by $50)
- When a project requires significantly more expertise than the rate reflects
- Never discount for "exposure" or "future work potential" — learned that lesson

**When to negotiate down:**
- Mission-aligned work I genuinely care about (rare)
- Long-term retainer with guaranteed volume (ClearPath, Bloom)
- First piece for a dream publication where the relationship matters more than the rate

**Current client rates:**
- Horizon Magazine: $0.50/word (feature rate)
- Bloom Studio: $300/post (retainer, 4 posts/month = $1,200/month)
- ClearPath: $400/post (retainer, 2 posts/month = $800/month)
- The Synthesis (own newsletter): $0 but building the audience and the portfolio

**Goal for end of 2026:** Raise feature rate to $0.55-0.65/word. Add one more retainer client at $500+/post. Get total monthly freelance income consistently above $5K.
