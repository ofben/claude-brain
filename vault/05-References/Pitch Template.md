## Pitch Notes to Self

- **Follow up:** If no response in 7-10 business days, send ONE polite follow-up. After that, move on.
- **Simultaneous pitches:** It's fine to pitch the same idea to multiple outlets IF the angles are genuinely different. Never send identical pitches to competing publications.
- **Research the editor:** Read their recent bylines, see what they've been commissioning, reference something specific they published. Shows you actually read the publication.
- **Keep it short:** The whole pitch should be readable in under 2 minutes. If the editor has to scroll, it's too long.
- **Don't attach clips:** Link to them. Attachments get flagged by spam filters and are annoying to open on mobile.
- **Track pitches:** Note what I pitched, where, when, and the outcome. Patterns emerge — what topics get picked up, which editors respond, what angles fall flat.
