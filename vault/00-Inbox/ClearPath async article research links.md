Research links for ClearPath async communication article

1. Buffer State of Remote Work 2025 report
   https://buffer.com/state-of-remote-work-2025
   Key stat: 67% of remote workers say too many meetings is their biggest productivity killer
   Good for the opening hook

2. Cal Newport's "A World Without Email" — summary/review on HBR
   https://hbr.org/2021/03/beyond-email-review
   His concept of "hyperactive hive mind" workflow — perfect framing for why async matters

3. GitLab's handbook on async communication
   https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/
   They're one of the biggest fully remote companies, good case study
   Could quote their "async-first" principles

4. Loom blog post about async video replacing meetings
   https://www.loom.com/blog/async-video-communication
   Biased source (they sell async video) but good data points about meeting fatigue

5. Microsoft Work Trend Index 2025
   https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index
   The "triple peak day" finding — people working in three bursts instead of 9-5
   Supports the argument that async lets people work in their natural rhythms
