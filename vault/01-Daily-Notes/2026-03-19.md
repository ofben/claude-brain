# Wednesday, March 19

Today felt different. I committed to actually using Obsidian as my main workspace. Spent the morning importing notes from Google Docs, Apple Notes, and that terrifying ideas.txt file. It's a mess but at least it's all in one place now.

## What I did
- Imported and organized maybe 60% of my scattered notes into this vault. Created some folder structure but trying not to over-organize before I understand how I actually want to use this.
- Worked on the [[Draft - Async Communication|ClearPath async article]] for a couple hours. Got the intro done and two solid sections drafted. The [[Remote Work Communication Research|research notes]] are actually useful when they're linkable — I could jump between the draft and my sources in a way that felt seamless.

- Tweaked the [[Draft - Newsletter Tools That Think|newsletter draft]] a bit. It's maybe 60% done. The personal angle about my own tool journey is working well — feels authentic because it IS authentic.

## Observations on this Obsidian experiment
Ok so the linking thing is real. I was writing the ClearPath article and referenced a stat from my research notes, and then noticed that same research note also connected to something I'd been thinking about for the Horizon piece. Ideas from different projects are cross-pollinating in a way they never could when they lived in separate Google Docs.

But I'm also aware of the trap. I've seen people on Reddit who spend months building elaborate Obsidian setups and never actually write anything. The system has to SERVE the writing, not replace it. I'm giving myself two weeks to see if this actually improves my output or if it's just another shiny tool.

What I like so far:
- Everything in one place (finally)
- Links between notes feel natural
- Markdown is simple and portable
- I own my files (no vendor lock-in)

What I'm worried about:
- Over-organizing / procrastinating via organizing
- Will I actually maintain this?
- Templates and structure — how much is enough?
- I don't fully understand the graph view thing yet
