## The note problem
I'm feeling the pain acutely today. I have:
- Research for 3 different client articles scattered across Google Docs
- Newsletter ideas in Apple Notes
- Client briefs in email
- Random thoughts in a text file on my desktop called "ideas.txt" that I'm afraid to open
- Bookmarks I've saved and never looked at again
- Voice memos from walks that I never transcribe

How does anyone build on their past thinking when it's spread across 6 different apps? This is exactly what the Zettelkasten people are talking about. Your ideas can't connect if they can't find each other.

I set up Obsidian over the weekend and moved a few things over. It's... promising? The linking between notes is exactly what I want. But I'm also wary of spending more time organizing notes than actually writing. The tool can't become the work.
