# Digital Boundaries Series — Overview

4-part blog series for Bloom Studio about creating healthier relationships with technology. Target audience: wellness-conscious professionals, 25-45, who feel overwhelmed by their devices but don't want to go full Luddite.

## The Posts

### Post 1: "The Notification Audit"
**Status:** Published (March 5, 2026)
About auditing and restructuring notifications. How to categorize notifications by urgency vs importance. Practical steps for each major app/platform.
*Performed well — 2x their average blog traffic.*

### Post 2: "Screen Time Is the Wrong Metric"
**Status:** Published (March 12, 2026)
Reframes the conversation away from screen time totals toward what actually matters: intentional vs. passive use. Evidence-backed argument for quality over quantity.
*Client feedback: loved the reframe, strong engagement.*

### Post 3: "The Case for Boring Phones"
**Status:** In draft — [[Draft - Digital Boundaries Post 3]]
The argument for stripping phones down to essentials. Practical guide to auditing apps, reducing friction for good habits, and increasing friction for bad ones.
*Due: March 20, 2026 — in final review.*

### Post 4: "Building a Digital Sunset Routine"
**Status:** Not started
Evening routines and the psychology of winding down. Not just "blue light is bad" but what we're really doing when we scroll before bed and how to design an intentional alternative.
*Due: March 27, 2026*
