# ClearPath — Content Guidelines

From onboarding doc and call with James Park (Content Lead), January 2026.

## About ClearPath
B2B SaaS platform for team communication and project management. Competitors: Slack, Asana, Monday.com. Their differentiator: built around async-first communication with optional sync features.

## Audience
- Team leads and managers at companies with 50-500 employees
- Often remote or hybrid teams
- Pain points: too many meetings, communication overload, difficulty coordinating across time zones
- They're already using some tools but frustrated with the current setup

## Content Goals
- Position ClearPath as thought leaders in async/remote work communication
- Drive organic traffic through SEO-optimized blog content
- Educate potential customers without being overtly salesy
- Build trust through genuinely useful, well-researched content

## SEO Requirements
- Primary keyword in title (H1)
- Primary keyword in first 100 words
- Primary keyword in at least 1 H2 subheading
- Secondary keywords sprinkled naturally throughout
- Meta description: 150-160 characters, include primary keyword
- Internal links to at least 2 other ClearPath blog posts (they'll provide URLs)
- At least 1 external link to authoritative source

## Formatting
- 1500-2000 words per post
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences max)
- H2 subheadings every 200-300 words
- Use bullet points and numbered lists where appropriate
- Include at least one "key takeaway" or summary box
- Bold key phrases for scannability
- End with clear CTA (they'll usually provide the specific CTA)

## Voice
- Professional but approachable
- Data-driven — include stats and research where possible
- Confident but not arrogant
- Practical and actionable — "here's what you can do Monday morning"
- Avoid: buzzwords without substance, overly casual tone, negativity about competitors

## Process
1. Receive topic and keyword from James
2. Submit outline for approval (usually 1-2 day turnaround)
3. Write first draft
4. Submit for review — they do SEO check and brand review
5. One round of revisions
6. Final approval and publish

## Payment
- $600 per post (1500-2000 words)
- Net 30 from invoice date
- Invoice after final approval
