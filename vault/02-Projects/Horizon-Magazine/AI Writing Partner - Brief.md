# The AI Writing Partner

## Assignment
Feature article for Horizon Magazine's spring issue exploring how writers are actually using AI tools in their creative process — beyond the hype and fear.

## Angle
Personal + reported piece. Start with my own experience using AI tools as a freelance writer, then widen to include other writers' perspectives. The goal is nuance: not "AI is amazing" and not "AI is destroying writing" but the messy reality in between.

Sarah wants this to feel like a conversation, not a report. "Show us your process" was her exact note from our [[Meeting notes Sarah 03-15|March 15 call]].

## Key Sections (working structure)
1. **The Promise** — Open with a personal anecdote about discovering AI writing tools. The excitement, the "holy shit" moment. (See [[Draft - The AI Writing Partner Opening]] for three attempts at this)
2. **The Reality Check** — What actually happens when you try to use AI for real writing work. The limitations, the weird outputs, the uncanny valley of AI prose.
3. **The Writers** — 3-4 interviews with writers at different points on the spectrum. Need at least one skeptic. (Devon Zhao for the skeptic angle?)
4. **The Middle Ground** — Where AI tools actually help: research, brainstorming, editing, getting unstuck. Where they don't: voice, original thinking, emotional resonance.
5. **Looking Forward** — What this means for the craft. Not predictions, but questions worth sitting with.
