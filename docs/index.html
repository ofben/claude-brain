The Solution

An AI tutor that teaches you and builds the system for you

Claude Brain isn't a video course or a PDF. It's an interactive AI experience — Claude becomes your personal tutor and builds your second brain step by step.

Type a command

Open Claude Code and type /start-1-1. Claude becomes your tutor — explaining concepts, answering questions, and guiding you through hands-on exercises.

Learn by doing

Every module works with your actual Obsidian vault. Claude reads real notes, finds real connections, and creates real files. No fake examples — everything is live.

Build real tools

By graduation, you have slash commands (/daily, /process-inbox), AI agents (The Librarian, The Critic, The Connector), and automated workflows you keep using forever.

AI Agents

Three specialists. One vault.

By Level 3, you'll have three AI agents with distinct personalities — each designed to help with a different aspect of knowledge management.

📚

The Librarian

"Methodical, slightly fussy, always kind"

Scans your vault for orphan notes, misplaced files, broken links, and tag inconsistencies. Produces structured Vault Health Reports and suggests specific fixes. Think of a librarian who notices a mishelved book and can't rest until it's fixed.

🔍

The Critic

"Rigorous but deeply constructive"

Reviews your drafts with honest, specific feedback. Finds logical gaps, unsupported claims, and weak reasoning — but always starts with what's working. Like a brilliant editor who makes your work better without breaking your spirit.

🕸️

The Connector

"Enthusiastic, thinks in webs"

Finds hidden relationships between ideas across your vault. Surfaces non-obvious connections, cross-project pollination, and recurring patterns. Like that friend who reads about architecture and immediately links it to your writing.
