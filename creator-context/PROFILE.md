# Alex Rivera — Creator Profile

## The Basics

- **Name:** Alex Rivera
- **Location:** Portland, OR
- **Role:** Freelance writer and content creator
- **Years independent:** 4 (since early 2022)
- **Previous:** Content Strategist at Greenline Digital (a mid-size digital marketing agency in Portland), 2 years. Left to go solo after realizing most of the interesting thinking was happening in side projects, not client decks.

## What I Write About

I work across four main niches, and the overlap between them is where most of my best ideas come from:

- **Technology** — Not gadget reviews. How tools shape the way we think, create, and work. AI, knowledge management, digital infrastructure.
- **Productivity** — But the human kind. Systems that actually hold up under real-world pressure. Workflows that adapt instead of breaking.
- **Creative work** — The craft and business of making things. Writing process, idea development, creative careers.
- **Wellness** — Specifically digital wellness. Boundaries with technology, sustainable work habits, the attention economy.

## The Synthesis (Newsletter)

My biweekly newsletter about creativity and technology. The core premise: the most interesting ideas live at the intersection of fields that don't usually talk to each other.

- **Subscribers:** ~1,200
- **Cadence:** Every other Saturday
- **Format:** One main essay (800-1,200 words) connecting 2-3 seemingly unrelated ideas, plus a short "signal" section with links and observations
- **Platform:** Substack
- **Growth:** Organic so far, mostly from Twitter/X threads and the occasional cross-promotion with other newsletter writers

## Online Presence

- **Twitter/X:** @alexrivera_writes — ~4,800 followers. Mostly threads about creative workflows, observations about AI tools, and the occasional rant about productivity culture. This is where most newsletter growth comes from.
- **LinkedIn:** Growing more intentionally here. Posting 2-3 times a week about freelancing, AI in content work, and the future of writing. ~2,100 connections. Gets more engagement per post than Twitter lately.
- **Personal site:** alexriverawrites.com — Portfolio, newsletter archive, contact page. Needs a redesign (has needed one for a year).

## How I Work

- **Hardware:** MacBook Pro 14" (M2), iPhone 15 for capture and quick notes
- **Writing:** Primarily in Obsidian now (3 months in), previously bounced between Notion, Evernote, Apple Notes, Google Docs, and plain text files. Nothing ever stuck for more than 6 months. Obsidian is the longest streak yet.
- **Notes situation:** Honestly, a mess. Ideas are scattered across at least 4 different apps and a physical notebook. I know what I've written about but can never find the specific note when I need it.
- **Research:** Readwise for highlights, Pocket for saving articles (thousands of unread items), browser bookmarks that are essentially a graveyard.
- **Task management:** Todoist, loosely. I track deadlines but not granular tasks.
- **Communication:** Slack with two clients, email with the third. My inbox is a secondary task manager whether I like it or not.
- **AI tools:** ChatGPT Plus, have experimented with Claude, Jasper (briefly, didn't stick), Midjourney for newsletter images. Curious but cautious — I use AI as a thinking partner, not a replacement for the writing itself.

## Strengths

- **Cross-domain thinking.** My best work comes from connecting an idea in one field to something in a completely different field. A concept from cognitive science applied to content strategy, or a principle from architecture applied to knowledge management. Clients hire me for this.
- **Voice flexibility.** I can write warm and personal for a wellness brand, then switch to authoritative and data-driven for a tech publication, and it all sounds natural.
- **Speed on first drafts.** Once I have a clear angle, I write fast. The bottleneck is never the writing itself.

## Weaknesses

- **Organization.** Notes everywhere, no single source of truth. I lose ideas because I can't find them. I've tried building systems but they always decay after a few weeks. This is the single biggest friction point in my work.
- **Saying no.** I take on projects because they're interesting, not because they fit into a sustainable workload. Leads to occasional deadline crunches.
- **Admin and business tasks.** Invoicing, contracts, tracking expenses — I do it, but always late and with resentment. Quarterly taxes are a recurring crisis.
- **Perfectionism on long-form.** First drafts come fast, but I can spend forever revising a feature article. Diminishing returns set in and I don't always notice.

## Background

- **Education:** B.A. in English, minor in Philosophy, University of Oregon
- **Early career:** Started as a copywriter at a small agency right out of college. Moved to Greenline Digital after a year. Worked my way up to Content Strategist, managing a team of 3 writers and overseeing content for about a dozen clients.
- **The leap:** Went freelance when a few clients from the agency offered to work with me directly. Had 3 months of runway saved. It was tight for the first year but has been sustainable since year two.
- **Other life:** Runs 3-4 times a week (training for a half marathon), reads widely (mostly nonfiction — science, philosophy, design), volunteers at a local writing workshop for high school students once a month.
