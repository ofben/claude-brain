## Horizon Magazine

> My anchor client and the work I'm proudest of. These features are portfolio pieces.

### Relationship Overview

- **Contact:** Sarah Chen, Editor-in-Chief
- **Email:** sarah.chen@horizonmag.com
- **Started:** September 2024 (18 months)
- **How we connected:** Sarah found one of my Twitter threads about AI writing tools, read my newsletter, and reached out. The first assignment was a trial piece on remote work culture. It did well, and we moved to a regular cadence.
- **Relationship health:** Strong. Sarah gives me a lot of creative latitude and trusts my instincts on angles. She pushes back when my drafts get too abstract, which makes the work better.

### Content Details

- **Type:** Long-form feature articles
- **Word count:** 2,000-3,500 words
- **Cadence:** 2 articles per month
- **Rate:** $0.50/word
- **Monthly value:** ~$2,500-3,500 depending on length
- **Focus areas:** AI tools and their real-world impact, future of work, the creator economy, digital culture
- **Style requirements:** Thoughtful and well-researched. Accessible to a general tech-interested audience — not just developers or insiders. No jargon dumps. Sarah's editorial standard: "Could a smart person who doesn't work in tech follow this and care?"
- **Process:** I pitch angles via email. Sarah approves or suggests adjustments. I submit a first draft, she gives one round of edits (usually structural + tone), I revise, it goes to copy editing. Turnaround from pitch to publish is usually 3-4 weeks.
- **CMS:** They use WordPress. I submit in Google Docs, their team handles formatting and upload.

- **Word count:** 1,500-2,000 words
- **Cadence:** 2 posts per month
- **Rate:** $400/post
- **Monthly value:** $800
